# Spanish tax system notes

Simplified model for planning purposes only.

## Tax bases

Spain distinguishes between:

- General income base
- Savings income base

## General income

Includes salaries, pensions, and pension plan redemptions.

## Savings income

Includes interest, dividends, and capital gains.

## Pension plans

Private pension plan contributions may reduce taxable general income up to legal limits.
Redemptions are taxed as general income.

## Investment funds

Capital gains are generally taxed in the savings base when sold.
Spanish fund-to-fund transfers may allow tax deferral, but the MVP can model sale taxation only.

## Important limitation

The model should allow rates and brackets to be configurable because legislation changes.
