# Investment product notes

## Remunerated account

- High liquidity.
- Interest taxed as savings income.
- Usually no market risk.
- Returns can change.

## Money market fund

- High liquidity.
- Low volatility.
- Not guaranteed.
- Gains taxed when sold.

## Investment fund

- Market risk.
- Tax deferral until sale.
- Fees matter significantly over decades.

## Pension plan

- Tax deduction today.
- Restricted liquidity.
- Redemption taxed as general income.
- Usefulness depends on current vs future tax rates and fees.

## Unit linked

- Insurance wrapper.
- Usually liquid or semi-liquid.
- No pension deduction.
- Gains taxed as savings income.
- Fees can be high.
