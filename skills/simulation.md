# Simulation skill

## Yearly simulation

Simulate from current year until retirement.

For each year:

1. Update age.
2. Update salary.
3. Calculate annual savings.
4. Allocate savings according to the strategy.
5. Apply mortgage payments and extra amortization.
6. Apply product returns and commissions.
7. Apply taxes when relevant.
8. Store yearly result.

## Required strategy outputs

- Gross wealth
- Net wealth after taxes
- Taxes paid
- Fees paid
- Mortgage balance
- Liquidity
- Inflation-adjusted wealth
