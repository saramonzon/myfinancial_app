# Investing skill

## Generic product parameters

Every product should be configurable through:

- Name
- Type
- Expected annual return
- Annual commission
- Tax treatment
- Liquidity
- Contribution limits
- Redemption assumptions

## Supported generic products

- Remunerated account
- Money market fund
- Investment fund
- Pension plan
- Unit linked

## Do not model bank-specific products

Use generic parameterized products so any real product can be evaluated by entering its conditions.
