# Dashboard skill

## Required dashboard views

- Strategy comparison
- Wealth over time
- Net wealth at retirement
- Taxes paid
- Fees paid
- Mortgage balance
- Liquidity
- Sensitivity analysis

## Preferred charts

- Line chart: wealth over time
- Bar chart: net wealth by strategy
- Bar chart: taxes and fees
- Line chart: mortgage balance
- Heatmap: commission vs return sensitivity

## Dashboard principles

- Keep assumptions visible.
- Show net values, not only gross values.
- Make differences between strategies explicit.
- Include warnings when assumptions drive results.
